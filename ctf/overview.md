# CTF Writeups

Challenges I solved myself. I write about how I got there, including the parts where I got nowhere, since that's usually the part worth reading.

## Format

1. What the challenge gives you
2. The detail that looks irrelevant and isn't
3. Approaches that failed, briefly
4. The approach that worked, with commands I actually ran
5. What carries over to the next challenge

## Notes

- No full flags for competitions that are still running
- Scripts live in this repo's `assets/` folder, not behind dead links
